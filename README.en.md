# Hi, I'm Tianyao

English · [中文](README.md)

I like turning recurring frustrations into things I can use. Lately, I've been tinkering with tools for reading papers, job hunting, and personal knowledge.

## Now

Testing whether what I take away from one paper can still be useful when I move on to the next.

## Three questions I'm chasing

### How do I keep papers from disappearing into PDFs and chat history?

[paper-deep-analysis-skill](https://github.com/Icey-Lan/paper-deep-analysis-skill) turns a PDF or arXiv paper into an offline reading report and structured data.

### Why should I have to reintroduce myself every time I look at a new role?

[JobBuff](https://github.com/Icey-Lan/JobBuff) keeps JD analysis, resume edits, and interview preparation in one task.

### Can a mess of job tabs become one list I can actually manage?

[job-hunter-web](https://github.com/Icey-Lan/job-hunter-web) collects job listings locally and tracks application progress.
